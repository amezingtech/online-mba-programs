Privacy Policy

Online MBA & Degrees — DPU Pune (the app) is published by Amezing Tech Academic Advisors, Satara. Last updated: October 2026.

1. What we collect

The app itself stores nothing on any server. All content (program details, fees, FAQs) is bundled inside the app. The only personal information we ever see is what you voluntarily type — your name, phone number, program interest and message — when you choose to contact us on WhatsApp.

2. How it is used

Your details are used only to respond to your admission inquiry via WhatsApp or phone, by our academic advisors. We do not sell, rent or share your information with any third party besides the university admission process you explicitly ask us to help with.

3. Third-party services

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — contact buttons open a WhatsApp chat; Meta's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.
  • Offline cache — the app caches its own pages on your device (service worker) so it works offline. This cache contains no personal data and can be cleared from your browser settings.

4. Children

The app describes university-level programs and is not directed at children under 13.
